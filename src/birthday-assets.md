# Birthday scenes

`birthday-night.webp` and `birthday-blow.webp` are AI-generated photorealistic
scenes created for this app with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-19
(Asia/Seoul). They depict a fictional adult couple looking at a cake beneath
the Milky Way, and the same woman leaning forward to blow out the candle.
They are not photographs of the user or any actual couple.

Both 1024 × 1536 PNG originals were encoded as WebP quality 88 for mobile
loading. A masked photo transition shows the woman's change of pose while
keeping the cake and sky steady. The candle, flame, breath, smoke and fireworks
are interactive HTML/CSS/SVG/canvas layers. Assets are served with the site;
no microphone or external image service is used at runtime.
