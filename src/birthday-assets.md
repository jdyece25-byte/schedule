# Birthday illustrations

`birthday-night.webp` and `birthday-blow.webp` are AI-generated hand-painted
illustrations created with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-19 (Asia/Seoul).
They depict fictional adults looking at a cake, and the woman leaning forward
to blow out a candle. These are original illustrated scenes, not photographs.

The 1024 × 1536 images are encoded as WebP quality 88. A masked transition
changes the woman's pose; HTML/CSS/SVG draw the candle, breath and smoke.
The canvas opening uses a world-space camera that follows a rising rocket,
parallax stars/clouds/mountains and staggered firework blooms before revealing
the couple. It lasts 7.8 seconds and can be skipped. Reduced-motion settings
bypass the opening and blowing animation. Extinguishing opens the original
letter automatically. Assets are local and no microphone is used.
