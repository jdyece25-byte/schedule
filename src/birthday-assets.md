# Birthday illustrations

The two WebP assets are original AI-generated cute cartoon illustrations made
with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-19 (Asia/Seoul). They depict fictional
adults, a woman with a chin-length bob, a friendly gray-and-white pit bull
terrier between the couple, a small cake, and a star-filled night
sky without a Milky Way. Each 1024 × 1536 source is encoded at WebP quality 88.

The app overlays four red interactive number candles (2026). The cake has no
caption. A single light/blow control is placed within the
scene. There is no separate letter/replay action bar. Lighting then blowing
out the candles opens the original letter automatically.

The 14.5-second skippable canvas opening follows a rocket before seven large
peony/willow firework shells spread across the viewport. Each shell has 130
sparks with bounded trails and cached glow sprites. A second volley forms
“22번째 / 생일 / 축하해” in three lines of gold, pink and blue particles,
holds briefly, then dissolves. Text is rasterized once using local Korean
fonts. Reduced motion skips the
opening and blowing animation. No microphone or external asset requests.
